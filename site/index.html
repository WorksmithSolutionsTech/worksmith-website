Our approach, in full →
  • 01

    Evidence before build

    Every recommendation traces to records in your own systems. If we cannot show you the underlying rows, we do not make the claim.

    Code, data, and infrastructure are yours. No proprietary runtime, no per-seat rent on your own operations, no exit penalty.

    A baseline is agreed in writing before the work starts, so the result at the end is a measurement rather than a story.

The order they belong in

Diagnose first. Fix the records if they need fixing. Automate last.

  1. Always first
    Vertical IBusiness Health Check

    A diagnostic can also conclude that no build is warranted.

  2. Then, if needed
    Vertical IIIDigital Foundation

    Only when the records underneath are unreliable or missing.

  3. Then
    Vertical IIWorkflow Automation

    Any earlier, and it makes an existing problem faster.

The honest version: Automation bought before the records exist is automation against unreliable data. We will say so at the diagnostic, not at go-live.
