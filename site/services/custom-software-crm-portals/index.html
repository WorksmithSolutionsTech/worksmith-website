How we build it

  • Workflow first — we map how the work moves today, including the informal steps, before designing a screen.
  • Audit trails by default — who changed what, when, and what it was before. Retrofitting this later is expensive and usually incomplete.
  • API-first — every entity reachable programmatically from day one, so analytics and automation are never a bolt-on.
  • Role-based access — designed against your actual approval authority, not a generic admin/user split.
  • Migration with reconciliation — historic data moved and then proved to tie back, rather than imported and hoped over.

What you gain

Structured, trustworthy data is the precondition for both other verticals. You cannot automate, monitor or forecast against a process that lives in a WhatsApp group and three spreadsheets named final_v2. This is the foundation — and building it correctly once is materially cheaper than building it twice, which is the usual outcome when the intelligence layer is bought first.
