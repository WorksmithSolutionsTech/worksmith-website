Where we start

Most operational loss is invisible because every system involved is working correctly.

That is why we do not open an engagement with software. We open it with measurement. Before anything is built, we establish what is actually leaking, what it costs in your own currency, and whether a system would fix it — because some of what we find is a process problem, and selling software for a process problem is how buyers end up paying twice.

Our approach, in full →
  • 01

    Evidence before build

    Every recommendation traces to records in your own systems. If we cannot show you the underlying rows, we do not make the claim.

  • 02

    You own what we build

    Code, data, and infrastructure are yours. No proprietary runtime, no per-seat rent on your own operations, no exit penalty.

  • 03

    Measured, not asserted

    A baseline is agreed in writing before the work starts, so the result at the end is a measurement rather than a story.

The order they belong in

Diagnose first. Fix the records if they need fixing. Automate last.

  1. Always first
    Vertical IBusiness Health Check

    Nothing else is priced honestly until the loss is measured. A diagnostic can also conclude that no build is warranted, and that is a valid result.

  2. Then, if needed
    Vertical IIIDigital Foundation

    If the diagnostic finds that the underlying records are unreliable or do not exist, the foundation is fixed before anything is automated on top of it.

  3. Then
    Vertical IIWorkflow Automation

    Automation applied to a process that is understood and a record layer that is trustworthy. Applied any earlier, it makes an existing problem faster.

The honest version: A business that buys Vertical II without Vertical III in place is paying us to automate against data that is not being captured reliably. We will tell you that at the diagnostic stage rather than at the go-live.
